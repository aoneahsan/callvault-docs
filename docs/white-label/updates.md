# Updates and renewals

An update is a new signed APK your users' app installs itself, along with the backup and web library changes that go with it. That covers capture fixes when Android or a phone maker changes what an app may hear, fixes and features released to CallVault during your update period, and security fixes for sign-in, backup and the admin dashboard.

## Included

Six months of updates from the day you pay, with a licence and with a buyout.

## Renewing

Renewals come in 3, 6 and 12 months. A renewal adds its months to the later of today and your current end date, so renewing early loses nothing. The prices are on the [updates page](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates), where you can also renew with your licence key and the email the licence was issued to.

## Without a renewal

Your app keeps running on the last version it received. Nothing is switched off, and your users' recordings stay where they are.
