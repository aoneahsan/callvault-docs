# White-label FAQ

## Do I get the source code with a licence?

No. A licence lets you offer CallVault under your name while we build and run it. The code changes hands only with a [buyout](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).

## Will my version be on Google Play?

We deliver it as your own signed APK, downloaded from your site, the way CallVault ships today. If you'd rather publish under your own store account, that account and its review are yours.

## Is there an iPhone version?

Not today. iPhone doesn't let an ordinary app record calls, so CallVault is built for Android.

## Can someone else license the same app?

Yes, until someone buys CallVault outright. After a buyout we stop selling it, and licences sold before it keep their terms.

## Is there anything to pay after a buyout?

Yes, a royalty. The buyer pays us 7% of the gross revenue CallVault earns, leaving out sales tax or VAT they collect. It's paid each quarter with a revenue statement, for as long as CallVault earns revenue, and it's still owed after the buyer sells CallVault on to someone else.

## Can you change the app for us?

Yes, as [custom work](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted and paid separately from the licence. Another language, different plans and limits, or importing a phone maker's recordings from a folder layout CallVault doesn't read yet are typical requests.

## How do I pay?

By Wise bank transfer. On the payment page you send the transfer to our Wise account and give us its reference; once we confirm it, your licence key and certificate arrive by email. Until online payment opens for CallVault, send the form on the [pricing page](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing) and we reply by email with the quote. Payment terms are in the [Terms of Service](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/terms#white-label).
