# Profile & preferences

Your profile and app preferences are available on both the phone (Settings → Profile) and
the [web app](/user-guide/web-app) (Profile tab).

## Profile

- See your **email** and **member-since** date.
- Edit your **display name**.

Your role and account status are managed separately and can only be changed by an
administrator — see [Roles & access](/admin-guide/roles).

## Appearance

Choose **light**, **dark**, or follow your system setting. On the web this preference is
remembered per browser.

## Language

CallVault is available in **English** and **Urdu (اردو)**. Urdu renders **right-to-left**,
and the whole interface — including the web app — mirrors accordingly. The app follows your
device or browser locale, and you can set it explicitly in preferences.

## Danger zone

The Profile screen is also where you delete your account. That's a bigger decision with
its own page — see [Account deletion](/user-guide/account-deletion).
