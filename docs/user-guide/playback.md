# Playback & speed

Tap any recording in the list to open its detail screen, where you can listen, adjust the
pace, and manage the file.

## Play a recording

The detail screen has an audio player with the usual play/pause and a seek bar. On the
phone it plays the local file; in the [web app](/user-guide/web-app) it streams the
backed-up copy from its URL. Direction (incoming/outgoing), number or contact, date,
duration, and your note are all shown alongside.

## Change the speed

Use the playback-speed control to slow a call down to catch a detail or speed it up to
review a long call quickly. The setting applies to the current playback session.

## Export, share, and save

From the detail screen you can:

- **Share** the audio to another app.
- **Save to Downloads** to keep a copy outside CallVault.
- **Bulk-select** several recordings in the list to export or delete them together.

Sharing and saving copy the audio out of CallVault's private storage — treat the exported
file the way you'd treat any sensitive recording, and keep in mind the
[call-recording-law notes](/getting-started/legal-disclaimer) if you plan to send it on.

## Delete

Deleting is covered on its own — you can remove a recording from just this device or from
everywhere. See [Account deletion](/user-guide/account-deletion#deleting-a-single-recording).
