# Deleting recordings & your account

CallVault gives you full control over removal — one recording at a time, or your entire
account.

## Deleting a single recording

From a recording's detail screen you choose the scope:

- **Delete on this device only** — removes the local file and its row from the phone, but
  keeps the backed-up copy in the cloud.
- **Delete everywhere** — removes the local copy, the metadata in Supabase, and the audio
  object in FilesHub.

## Deleting your account

Under **Profile → danger zone** (on the phone or the [web app](/user-guide/web-app)) you
can delete your whole account yourself. This is self-service — you don't have to ask anyone.

Deleting your account removes:

- your **sign-in identity**,
- your **profile** row,
- all of your **recording metadata**, and
- your backed-up **audio objects** in FilesHub.

Local files already on a device are removed by the app on that device; a copy you
previously **exported or saved to Downloads** lives outside CallVault and is not touched
by account deletion — remove those yourself.

:::warning This is permanent
Account deletion can't be undone. If you only want to stop backing up, sign out or turn
backup off instead — that keeps your recordings on the phone.
:::
