# Search & favorites

CallVault groups recordings by day and gives you a few ways to find and organise them,
on both the phone and the [web app](/user-guide/web-app).

## Search

Search by **number, contact name, or note**. On the phone there's a dedicated Search tab;
in the web app the search box lives at the top of the list. Your recent searches are
remembered.

## Filters

Narrow the list — for example to a direction or to favorites — from the filter control.
In the web app the active filter and search term are kept in the URL (`?filter=` and
`?q=`), so a refresh or a shared link restores exactly what you were looking at.

## Favorites and notes

- **Favorite** a call to keep it easy to find.
- **Add a note** to a recording from its detail screen — useful for what the call was
  about or a follow-up.

Both the favorite flag and the note **sync across your devices** when you're signed in,
using last-write-wins: the most recent change on any device is the one that sticks.
