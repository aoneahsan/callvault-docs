# Plans and Family

Plans change private cloud backup and nothing else. Recording, import, playback, search, notes, favorites and export are free on every plan.

## How much cloud storage do I get?

Free includes 2 GB, Pro 20 GB and Family 30 GB for each member; an administrator can raise a limit.

| Plan | Price | Cloud backup | Per recording | Uploads an hour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 0 | 2 GB | 100 MB | 60 |
| Pro | 4.99 USD a month or 39 USD a year | 20 GB | 100 MB | 300 |
| Family | 9.99 USD per seat a month or 79 USD per seat a year; 3 seats minimum | 30 GB for each member | 100 MB | 600 |

Every number there sits on a row an administrator maintains. They are the values CallVault launches with, not constants compiled into the app, so an administrator can raise a limit for a single account without shipping a new build.

## How do I request a paid plan?

1. Sign in on the [CallVault website](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com). Choose Pro or Family and select a month or year. For Family, create a household you own and choose 3 to 5 seats.
2. Submit the request. CallVault shows the amount and a pending status; submitting it does not charge you or activate a plan.
3. An administrator sends payment instructions privately and, after independently verifying payment outside the request, confirms the request and applies a dated plan to your CallVault account or household.
4. Check **Profile** for the active plan and its end date. The website shows request status.

Nothing is sold inside the Android app. A confirmed manual period does not renew automatically. When a plan ends, new backups pause at the lower limit and nothing already stored is deleted; you can request another period.

## How does the Family plan work?

One person owns the household and invites the others by email. A household holds up to 5 members. Family requests start at 3 paid seats, even if fewer people have joined; the owner must create the household before requesting the plan. Each member then gets the Family limits, including 30 GB of cloud backup. Each signs in with their own Google account, the only sign-in method.

## Leaving a household

Leave or be removed, and the membership goes at once. You return to your own plan or to Free immediately: nothing stored is deleted, and new backups pause at the lower limit. The household keeps its plan until its end date, even below 3 members.

The owner can't leave. The owner can delete the household instead, which removes every membership and leaves every recording where it is.

Your plan and its end date are in **Profile**. What each limit means day to day is in [private cloud backup](/user-guide/sync-backup).
