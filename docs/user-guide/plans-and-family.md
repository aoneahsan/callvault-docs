# Plans and Family

Plans change private cloud backup and nothing else. Recording, import, playback, search, notes, favorites and export are free on every plan.

## How much cloud storage do I get?

Free includes 2 GB, Pro 20 GB and Family 30 GB for each member; an administrator can raise a limit.

| Plan | Price | Cloud backup | Per recording | Uploads an hour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 0 | 2 GB | 100 MB | 60 |
| Pro | 4.99 USD a month or 39 USD a year | 20 GB | 100 MB | 300 |
| Family | 9.99 USD a month or 79 USD a year | 30 GB for each member | 100 MB | 600 |

Every number there sits on a row an administrator maintains. They are the values CallVault launches with, not constants compiled into the app, so an administrator can raise a limit for a single account without shipping a new build.

## How a paid plan reaches your account

1. Pay on the website at [aoneahsan.com/payment](https://aoneahsan.com/payment).
2. An administrator applies the plan to your account with an end date.
3. Check **Profile** for that end date.

Nothing is sold inside the Android app. A renewal extends that end date and never shortens it. When a plan ends, new backups pause at the lower limit and nothing already stored is deleted; pay again and an administrator re-applies the plan.

## How does the Family plan work?

One person owns the household and invites the others by email. A household holds up to 5 members, and Family is bought with at least 3 seats; every member then gets the Family limits, including 30 GB of cloud backup each. Each one signs in with their own Google account. It's the only sign-in method.

## Leaving a household

Leave or be removed, and the membership goes at once. You return to your own plan or to Free immediately: nothing stored is deleted, and new backups pause at the lower limit. The household keeps its plan until its end date, even below 3 members.

The owner can't leave. The owner can delete the household instead, which removes every membership and leaves every recording where it is.

Your plan and its end date are in **Profile**. What each limit means day to day is in [private cloud backup](/user-guide/sync-backup).
