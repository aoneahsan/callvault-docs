# The web library

Play what you backed up from any browser. The web library shows only backed-up recordings, so a phone full of calls can sit beside an empty list. It's a view of your cloud copy rather than a second recorder, and it keeps no library of its own.

## Sign in

1. Open [callvault.aoneahsan.com](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com).
2. Choose **Sign in with Google**. It's the only sign-in method.
3. Use the same account the phone uses.

## What you can do here

- Play any recording you backed up.
- Read **Stats**: total recordings, how many are backed up, total talk time, incoming versus outgoing counts, and a bar chart of recordings per day for the last 14 days.
- Open **Profile** for the **Private cloud backup** switch, your [preferences](/user-guide/profile) and the current plan with its end date.
- Delete a recording everywhere from its detail view.
- [Delete your account](/user-guide/account-deletion) and its cloud files.

## Before anything appears here

Private cloud backup is off until you turn it on, and one upload has to finish. Uploads run on Wi-Fi by default, and a call reaches this library only after its file has finished uploading. That switch is the first gate.

Turn it on from **Profile** here, or from **Settings → Storage & sync** on the phone.

## What it won't show

Anything that never left the phone. A recording ends up like that for one of three reasons: backup was off when you made it, its file hasn't finished uploading, or it is over the 100 MB per-recording limit. There is no per-recording "keep local" switch to look for.

## If the list is empty

Check the switch first. Then check the account. An empty library usually means private cloud backup has never been on, and the next most likely reason is that you signed in with a different Google account from the one on the phone.
