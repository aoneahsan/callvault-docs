# Moving from Talker ACR to CallVault

A call recorder from Joicoo Limited that also covers internet calls, with Free and Premium levels. Facts about it come from <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.talker.acr&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">its own page</a>, last checked 7 November 2026. Prices and the wider comparison are on [the CallVault site](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/compare/talker-acr).

## Should you move?

Stay on Talker ACR if one of these is you:

- You need WhatsApp, Viber, Skype, Slack or WeChat calls recorded: its listing names them, with the note that not all devices support it.

Move if these matter more:

- Backup on the free plan: Talker lists cloud backup under Premium, and CallVault’s Free plan includes 2 GB.
- A web library for your backed-up recordings.
- No ads, on any plan.

## How do you move?

1. [Install CallVault](/getting-started/install) and record one real call. Play it back before you change anything else. What CallVault hears depends on your phone.
2. Keep Talker ACR's recordings where they are until that test call sounds right.
3. To bring old recordings across, [point CallVault at the folder they're in](/user-guide/recording#import-from-another-recorder). CallVault reads those files and never changes them. It's built around phone makers' recorders and BCR-compatible ones, and we haven't tested Talker ACR's files, so try one first.
