# Moving from Automatic Call Recorder Pro to CallVault

A call recorder from SMSROBOT LTD. Its Google Play listing shows a last update of 1 November 2022. Facts about it come from <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.smsrobot.callu&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">its own page</a>, last checked 7 November 2026. Prices and the wider comparison are on [the CallVault site](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/compare/automatic-call-recorder-pro).

## Should you move?

Stay on Automatic Call Recorder Pro if one of these is you:

- You want to install from Google Play and share recordings by hand to Dropbox, Google Drive or a messaging app.

Move if these matter more:

- Updated this year. Its listing last changed in November 2022.
- Automatic backup to private storage, with a web library.
- No ads, on any plan.

## How do you move?

1. [Install CallVault](/getting-started/install) and record one real call. Play it back before you change anything else. What CallVault hears depends on your phone.
2. Keep Automatic Call Recorder Pro's recordings where they are until that test call sounds right.
3. To bring old recordings across, [point CallVault at the folder they're in](/user-guide/recording#import-from-another-recorder). CallVault reads those files and never changes them. It's built around phone makers' recorders and BCR-compatible ones, and we haven't tested Automatic Call Recorder Pro's files, so try one first.
