# Moving from Truecaller to CallVault

A caller ID and spam blocker with call recording listed under its Premium plan. Facts about it come from <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.truecaller&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">its own page</a>, last checked 7 November 2026. Prices and the wider comparison are on [the CallVault site](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/compare/truecaller).

## Should you move?

Stay on Truecaller if one of these is you:

- You want caller ID and spam blocking in the same app.
- You want an automatic summary and a written transcript of a call: its listing includes both, and CallVault’s are not switched on yet.

Move if these matter more:

- A recorder and nothing else: it doesn’t need to identify who is calling you, and never reads your address book.
- No ads, on any plan.
- A web library for your backed-up recordings.

## How do you move?

1. [Install CallVault](/getting-started/install) and record one real call. Play it back before you change anything else. What CallVault hears depends on your phone.
2. Keep Truecaller's recordings where they are until that test call sounds right.
3. To bring old recordings across, [point CallVault at the folder they're in](/user-guide/recording#import-from-another-recorder). CallVault reads those files and never changes them. It's built around phone makers' recorders and BCR-compatible ones, and we haven't tested Truecaller's files, so try one first.
