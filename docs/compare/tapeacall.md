# Moving from TapeACall to CallVault

A subscription call recorder with transcripts, from Mosaic Srl. Facts about it come from <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tapeacall.com&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">its own page</a>, last checked 7 November 2026. Prices and the wider comparison are on [the CallVault site](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/compare/tapeacall).

## Should you move?

Stay on TapeACall if one of these is you:

- You want transcripts today: its listing offers them, and CallVault’s are not switched on yet.
- You want recordings pushed to Dropbox, Evernote or Google Drive.

Move if these matter more:

- Recording costs nothing: TapeACall requires a subscription after a 7-day trial, and CallVault’s plans only change backup space.
- Backup is built in, so there’s no Dropbox or Drive account to connect, and the copies play in a browser.

## How do you move?

1. [Install CallVault](/getting-started/install) and record one real call. Play it back before you change anything else. What CallVault hears depends on your phone.
2. Keep TapeACall's recordings where they are until that test call sounds right.
3. To bring old recordings across, [point CallVault at the folder they're in](/user-guide/recording#import-from-another-recorder). CallVault reads those files and never changes them. It's built around phone makers' recorders and BCR-compatible ones, and we haven't tested TapeACall's files, so try one first.
