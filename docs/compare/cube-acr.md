# Moving from Cube ACR to CallVault

A call recorder that also covers internet calls such as WhatsApp, from Cube Apps Ltd. Facts about it come from <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.catalinagroup.callrecorder&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">its own page</a>, last checked 7 November 2026. Prices and the wider comparison are on [the CallVault site](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/compare/cube-acr).

## Should you move?

Stay on Cube ACR if one of these is you:

- You need WhatsApp, Viber, Google Meet or Line calls recorded: its listing names them and CallVault does not record internet calls.
- You want to install from Google Play instead of downloading an APK.

Move if these matter more:

- Cube ACR’s Play listing says it contains ads. CallVault has none, free or paid.
- Your old calls can come along: CallVault imports from a recorder’s folder, though we haven’t tested Cube ACR’s files yet.
- Backups play in a browser, at work or at home. Its listing doesn’t mention that.

## How do you move?

1. [Install CallVault](/getting-started/install) and record one real call. Play it back before you change anything else. What CallVault hears depends on your phone.
2. Keep Cube ACR's recordings where they are until that test call sounds right.
3. To bring old recordings across, [point CallVault at the folder they're in](/user-guide/recording#import-from-another-recorder). CallVault reads those files and never changes them. It's built around phone makers' recorders and BCR-compatible ones, and we haven't tested Cube ACR's files, so try one first.
