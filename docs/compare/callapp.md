# Moving from CallApp to CallVault

A caller ID, spam blocking and messaging app that includes an automatic call recorder. Facts about it come from <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.callapp.contacts&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">its own page</a>, last checked 7 November 2026. Prices and the wider comparison are on [the CallVault site](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/compare/callapp).

## Should you move?

Stay on CallApp if one of these is you:

- You want caller ID, spam blocking and a default SMS app along with recording.

Move if these matter more:

- It doesn’t need to be your SMS app or read your address book. It records calls and that’s the whole job.
- CallApp’s Play listing says it contains ads. Nothing in CallVault is ad-supported.
- Its listing doesn’t mention backing recordings up. CallVault keeps a private copy, if you want one, that plays in a browser.

## How do you move?

1. [Install CallVault](/getting-started/install) and record one real call. Play it back before you change anything else. What CallVault hears depends on your phone.
2. Keep CallApp's recordings where they are until that test call sounds right.
3. To bring old recordings across, [point CallVault at the folder they're in](/user-guide/recording#import-from-another-recorder). CallVault reads those files and never changes them. It's built around phone makers' recorders and BCR-compatible ones, and we haven't tested CallApp's files, so try one first.
