# Moving from Phone by Google to CallVault

Google’s own phone app, with call recording on certain devices and carriers. Facts about it come from <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.dialer&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">its own page</a>, last checked 7 November 2026. Prices and the wider comparison are on [the CallVault site](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/compare/phone-by-google).

## Should you move?

Stay on Phone by Google if one of these is you:

- You want everyone on the call to hear that it is being recorded: Google’s app announces it, and CallVault does not.
- Your phone and country support it, and a copy on the phone is all you need.

Move if these matter more:

- Backup, if you want a copy that survives a lost phone. Google says its recordings are not saved or backed up off the device.
- It isn’t limited to Google’s list of devices and carriers, though what it hears still depends on your phone.

## How do you move?

1. [Install CallVault](/getting-started/install) and record one real call. Play it back before you change anything else. What CallVault hears depends on your phone.
2. Keep Phone by Google's recordings where they are until that test call sounds right.
3. To bring old recordings across, [point CallVault at the folder they're in](/user-guide/recording#import-from-another-recorder). CallVault reads those files and never changes them. It's built around phone makers' recorders and BCR-compatible ones, and we haven't tested Phone by Google's files, so try one first.

## How do they differ?

| What it does | CallVault | Phone by Google |
|---|---|---|
| Records phone calls on Android | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/9803950?hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) |
| Starts recording by itself | Yes | Not stated on their site |
| Records WhatsApp and other internet calls | No | Not stated on their site |
| The other person is clear without the speaker | Partly | Not stated on their site |
| Announces the recording on the call | No | Yes (<a href="https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/9803950?hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) |
| Works without rooting the phone | Yes | Not stated on their site |
| Installs from Google Play | No | Yes (<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.dialer&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) |
| Says recordings stay on the phone | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/9803950?hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) |
| Copies recordings off the phone | Yes | No (<a href="https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/9803950?hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) |
| Plays recordings in a web library | Yes | No (<a href="https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/9803950?hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">source</a>) |
| Imports recordings other apps made | Yes | Not stated on their site |
| Writes a transcript of the call | No | Not stated on their site |
| Shows no ads | Yes | Not stated on their site |
| Open source | No | Not stated on their site |
