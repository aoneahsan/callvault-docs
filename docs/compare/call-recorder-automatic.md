# Moving from Call Recorder Automatic to CallVault

A call recorder from Tap AI with caller ID and an ignore list. Facts about it come from <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=il.co.smedia.callrecorder.yoni&amp;hl=en_US&amp;gl=US" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">its own page</a>, last checked 7 November 2026. Prices and the wider comparison are on [the CallVault site](https://callvault.aoneahsan.com/compare/call-recorder-automatic).

## Should you move?

Stay on Call Recorder Automatic if one of these is you:

- You want to install from Google Play and have caller ID in the same app.

Move if these matter more:

- No ads, on any plan.
- A web library for your backed-up recordings.
- Import from another recorder’s folder.

## How do you move?

1. [Install CallVault](/getting-started/install) and record one real call. Play it back before you change anything else. What CallVault hears depends on your phone.
2. Keep Call Recorder Automatic's recordings where they are until that test call sounds right.
3. To bring old recordings across, [point CallVault at the folder they're in](/user-guide/recording#import-from-another-recorder). CallVault reads those files and never changes them. It's built around phone makers' recorders and BCR-compatible ones, and we haven't tested Call Recorder Automatic's files, so try one first.
