The first public release of CallVault — a personal Android call recorder that keeps your
calls offline-first and, if you want, backs them up to storage you control.

<!-- truncate -->

## Highlights

- **Record your own calls** automatically, with an honest account of what a stock Android
  phone can and can't capture.
- **Play back** with speed controls, and **search** your history by number, contact, or note.
- **Favorites and notes** that sync across your devices when you're signed in.
- **Optional backup** to storage you control — offline-first, so nothing is lost if you're
  offline.

CallVault isn't on Google Play (Play bans silent call recorders); install it by
[sideloading the APK](/getting-started/install).
