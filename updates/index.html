CallVault now opens to your own scripts and assistants, read only

November 22, 2026 · 2 min read
The CallVault team

You can now point your own tools at your CallVault account. A backup script can read your recordings with an access key, Claude or ChatGPT can answer "How many calls did I record last week?", and your server can hear the moment a recording's audio finishes uploading.

CallVault can now run under your name

November 13, 2026 · One min read
The CallVault team

A business can now offer CallVault to its own users under its own name. We build the Android app with that business's name, icon and colours, put the download page on its domain, and keep running backup, the admin dashboard and the updates behind it.

Three things stand out in 1.3.0, a prerelease for device testing. A cloud copy is now a private file, a call opens as the separate tracks CallVault found, and one screen tells you whether capture is ready before the call that matters.
